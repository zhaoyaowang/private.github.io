童画 · 隐私政策

适用于「童画－AI 照片创作」商用 iOS 版。更新于 2026 年 11 月 24 日。

选照片、授权与 AI 处理

你用系统照片选择器挑选一张图片。选择、预览和调整在本机完成。童画不扫描或备份相册。在包含上传授权的版本中，首次提交前会说明处理方式并征求同意；你可以拒绝并继续浏览，也可在设置中撤回今后上传的授权。

提交后，选定照片和成片存放在腾讯云广州私有对象存储（COS）。新加坡后台调度任务，并可能下载、校验及回存图片。服务根据连接 IP 判断线路；中国大陆线路使用火山引擎方舟 Seedream，海外线路使用 OpenAI。所选线路的模型服务商会收到照片和编辑指令，用于生成这次作品。手机也可能直接从模型服务商的临时地址下载结果。使用原始文件时，文件可能含相机信息或位置信息。可先在系统中移除这些信息。

请只上传你有权处理的照片。照片含他人尤其是儿童时，请先取得适当授权或监护人同意。童画用照片生成作品，不建立人脸识别账户或用于身份认证的面部模板。保存作品时才请求向系统相册添加图片的权限。

账户、购买和运行记录

通过 Apple 登录时，童画用应用专属的 Apple 账户标识摘要及内部账户编号管理会话。Apple 处理付款。童画处理商品和交易编号、订阅状态、积分流水及其关联任务，以交付权益、恢复购买和核对退款。童画无法读取你的支付卡号。

为分析滤镜体验、购买流程和故障，服务接收有限的操作事件与诊断数据，如滤镜、比例、提交、查看、保存、购买操作、任务编号、错误类别、文件大小及阶段耗时。任务本身需要保存编辑指令；这些指令和图片不会写进行为统计。童画不为跨 App 广告追踪使用这些数据。

保留、删除与联系

任务输入、输出、指令和临时副本用于生成、恢复和下载；当前没有面向所有任务的固定自动到期天数。请将想保留的作品保存到系统相册。注销后关闭账户和会话，清除账户关联的任务与余额，后台排队删除由童画控制的云端作品和原图。正在处理的请求、已签发链接、故障重试及备份轮替可能使清理延后。服务商自行保留的数据受其条款约束。必要的交易核对、防止重复发放或依法留存的记录可能另行保留。

新版入口为「设置 → 删除账户」。也可以通过下方邮箱私下提出访问、更正或删除请求。删除账户不会取消 Apple 的自动续订；请在系统订阅设置中另行管理。已保存到系统相册的图片由你自己管理。

TongHua Privacy Policy

Commercial iOS app, TongHua – AI Photo Creation. Updated November 24, 2026.

Choosing and sharing a photo

You choose a photo with Apple's system picker. Selection, preview and composition take place on your device. TongHua does not scan or back up your library. Versions with the upload permission screen ask before your first submission. You can decline and continue browsing, or withdraw permission for future uploads in Settings.

When you submit, Tencent Cloud Object Storage (COS) in Guangzhou stores your chosen image and result privately. Our Singapore service coordinates jobs and may download, verify and archive files. Your connection IP determines the processing route. The mainland China route uses Volcengine Ark Seedream; the overseas route uses OpenAI. The selected provider receives the image and editing instructions to create your result. Your device may download a result directly from a temporary provider link. Original image files may contain camera metadata or location. You can remove this information on your device before sharing.

Only submit photos you have permission to process. Obtain appropriate permission for other people, including a parent or guardian's permission where applicable for children. TongHua uses photos to create images and does not build facial recognition profiles or face templates for authentication. Saving a result requests permission to add an image to your Photos library.

Accounts, purchases and diagnostics

Sign in with Apple provides an app-specific account identifier. TongHua uses its digest and an internal account ID to manage sessions. Apple handles payments. We process product and transaction IDs, subscription status, credits and related job records to deliver purchases, restore entitlements and reconcile refunds. We do not receive your payment card number.

To understand filter use, purchasing and failures, we collect limited interactions and diagnostics, such as filter, aspect ratio, submit, view, save and purchase events, job IDs, error categories, file sizes and elapsed time. Editing instructions are required for your job and are kept out of behavioral analytics, along with your images. These data are not used for cross-app advertising tracking.

Retention, deletion and contact

Job inputs, outputs, instructions and temporary copies support generation, recovery and downloads. There is currently no fixed automatic expiry for all jobs. Save the images you want to keep to Photos. Account deletion closes the account and sessions, removes its job records and credits, and queues removal of its inputs and results from storage we control. In-flight operations, short-lived links, retries and backup rotation may delay removal. Providers may retain data under their own policies. Records needed for transaction reconciliation, preventing duplicate credit or legal obligations may be retained separately.

In updated versions, open Settings → Delete account. You may also email us privately to request access, correction or deletion. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage renewal in Apple's subscription settings. Copies saved to your Photos library remain under your control.

Developer and privacy contact: Zhaoyao Wang, wzystars@outlook.com. Please do not email photos, passwords or verification codes.
